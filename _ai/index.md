<section class="brand-section" id="solutions" aria-labelledby="solutions-title">
<p class="eyebrow">What we can build together</p>
<h2 id="solutions-title">AI solutions connected to your work.</h2>
<div class="brand-grid two">
<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### Company knowledge & enterprise search

**Make company information easier to find and use.** Give your team an assistant that answers questions using your own documents, with references back to the sources.

We build knowledge assistants and document Q&A systems over PDFs, knowledge repositories, and supported platforms such as SharePoint, Confluence, and Google Drive. The work includes document processing, indexing, hybrid keyword and semantic search, and relevance tuning.

Retrieval follows the access permissions agreed for the integration. We evaluate both the information retrieved and the answers generated, so quality can be checked and improved.

*Example: help an operations team find the right policy or procedure, with a link to the original document.*


</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### AI agents & controlled workflows

**Move a defined task forward with the right tools and oversight.** Task-specific agents can gather information, prepare documents, and coordinate steps across supported applications.

We define tool permissions, action limits, audit trails, and human approval points around the workflow. Connections to CRM, ERP, or internal systems depend on available APIs, access rights, and the constraints of your environment. More complex agent workflows are considered when the use case justifies them.

*Example: gather account information and prepare an internal brief for a team member to review.*


</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### Business process automation

**Spend less time moving information between systems.** We combine conventional automation, APIs, and AI where they help with repetitive work.

Use cases include email classification and routing, invoice and document processing, support ticket triage, data entry and validation, reporting workflows, and lead qualification. Approval steps, exception handling, and monitoring are part of the workflow design.

*Example: extract fields from incoming documents, validate them, and send exceptions to a person for review.*


</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### Conversational AI & assistants

**Help customers and employees get useful answers in context.** We build conversational interfaces connected to relevant business information and agreed workflows.

Solutions include internal employee assistants, website and customer support assistants, product guidance, multilingual interfaces, and domain-specific copilots. We agree the assistant's scope, evaluate its responses, and define when it should hand over to a person.

*Example: answer common product questions using approved documentation and route unresolved requests to your support team.*


</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### AI for data & analytics

**Make business data easier to explore and explain.** We connect AI-assisted analysis to defined metrics and validated data, so conversations stay grounded in the business.

Solutions can include conversational analytics, natural-language-to-SQL with controlled database access, KPI exploration, business summaries, anomaly reporting, and explanations of data quality issues. Generated queries and summaries need validation against agreed definitions and expected results.

*Example: explore a change in a KPI and prepare a business summary linked to the underlying figures.*


</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### Data engineering & business intelligence

**Build the data foundations that analytics and AI depend on.** We also offer data engineering and BI as standalone services, whether or not you are ready for an AI application.

We integrate databases, APIs, and business systems; build ingestion, cleaning, and transformation pipelines; define KPIs and data models; and prepare reliable data for dashboards and operational reporting. Data quality checks help teams understand whether the information is ready to use.

*Example: bring operational data into a consistent reporting model before building a dashboard or analytics assistant.*


</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### Evaluation, monitoring & ongoing improvement

**Keep AI applications useful after the prototype.** We define evaluation cases, test retrieval and answer quality, and plan monitoring for production use.

The work can cover logging, alerts, latency and cost monitoring, access controls, audit trails, and ongoing maintenance. Cloud, hybrid, or on-premises deployment options are assessed against your systems, privacy needs, and operational requirements.

*Example: test a knowledge assistant against representative questions and track quality as documents and models change.*


</article>
