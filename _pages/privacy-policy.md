Controller contact details:

## 2. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal data when you:

- visit or interact with a Data Conscious Labs landing page or form.
- join a waitlist, request information, or contact us.
- enroll in or pay for a course, cohort, lab, or related learning product.
- use Moodle, GitHub-based learner materials, technical lab access, or other learning systems connected to our services.
- receive operational, support, or marketing communications from us.

This policy does not replace the privacy notices of third-party services that may be used to deliver parts of the learning experience, such as Stripe, MailerLite, MailerSend, GitHub, or other providers.

## 3. Personal data we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following categories of personal data.

### Identity and contact information

This may include your name, email address, company name, role, country, and any other information you provide when registering, joining a waitlist, making an inquiry, or enrolling.

### Payment and billing information

Payments may be processed through Stripe or another payment provider. We may receive information such as payment status, transaction identifiers, customer email, billing details, invoice details, VAT or tax details where applicable, and other information needed to confirm enrollment or keep accounting records. We do not intentionally collect or store full payment card numbers.

### Course registration and learner account information

This may include your enrollment status, cohort, course access status, Moodle account details, GitHub or learner repository access information, lab usernames, provisioning status, and access records for learning tools used as part of the course.

### Learning platform usage information

We may process information about course participation, Moodle activity, submitted work, support needs, live-session participation, lab progress, account access, and use of technical learning environments where this is needed to deliver or support the course.

### Technical and log data

Our systems and service providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device information, login events, security logs, error logs, claim-link status, access timestamps, and other operational records needed to run and secure the service.

### Communication and support data

If you contact us by email, forms, replies to onboarding messages, or other support channels, we may process the contents of those communications and related metadata.

### Marketing consent and preferences

If you subscribe to updates, join a waitlist, or opt in to marketing communications, we may process your marketing preferences, subscription status, email engagement information, and unsubscribe choices.

## 4. How we collect personal data

We may collect personal data:

- directly from you when you complete forms, enroll, pay, contact us, or use the course.
- through landing pages, waitlists, and email tools such as MailerLite or MailerSend.
- through payment providers such as Stripe when you complete checkout or payment.
- through Moodle, GitHub, Codespaces, Cloudflare-protected access routes, and other learning or lab systems.
- through automated technical logs generated by our infrastructure and service providers.
- from your communications with us before, during, or after a course.

We do not receive the email address or direct identifying details of every anonymous landing-page visitor. We receive personal data when you submit a form, subscribe, pay, contact us, enroll, request support, or otherwise provide identifying information.

## 5. Why we use personal data

We use personal data for practical course and business purposes, including to:

- manage inquiries, waitlists, enrollments, and course access.
- process payments, confirm payment status, issue invoices, and keep accounting records.
- provide Moodle access, learner materials, live sessions, technical labs, and related course resources.
- provision and validate learner accounts for course tools where applicable.
- send operational emails, onboarding instructions, technical-access messages, course updates, and support responses.
- provide support and troubleshoot account, payment, or technical access issues.
- protect accounts, infrastructure, and learning systems from misuse or unauthorized access.
- improve the course experience, learning materials, onboarding process, and support operations.
- comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.
- send marketing communications only where lawful and appropriate, including where you have consented or where another lawful basis applies.

## 6. Legal bases under the GDPR

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases.

- Contract necessity: to enroll you, provide course access, process course-related requests, deliver learning materials, and operate the services you requested.
- Legal obligation: to comply with tax, accounting, invoicing, consumer, and regulatory requirements.
- Legitimate interests: to operate and improve our learning business, secure our systems, respond to support requests, manage limited cohort access, and maintain appropriate records, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.
- Consent: for optional marketing communications, optional cookies or tracking where required, and other processing where we ask for consent.

We may use service providers and technical tools to deliver the course and operate the business. These may include:

- MailerLite and MailerSend for landing pages, email lists, automations, and transactional or marketing emails.

## 8. International transfers

Some service providers may process personal data outside your country or outside the European Economic Area. Where required, we expect appropriate safeguards to be used, such as adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

## 9. Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or allowed by law.

Typical retention categories include:

- enrollment, account, and learner access records for as long as needed to provide the course, maintain agreed access, resolve support issues, and keep appropriate business records.
- payment, billing, invoice, tax, and accounting records for the period required by applicable law.
- support communications for as long as needed to respond, resolve issues, and maintain a reasonable history of the request.
- marketing records until you unsubscribe, withdraw consent, or the records are no longer needed.
- technical, security, and audit logs for a limited period appropriate to security, troubleshooting, and operational needs.
- inactive, expired, or unused lab access records when they are no longer needed for course delivery, security, or legal purposes.

## 10. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. These may include access controls, password-free or one-time access flows where appropriate, encryption of sensitive credentials, least-privilege service access, audit logging, HTTPS-protected access, and operational safeguards for learner infrastructure.

No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Contractual responsibilities for
using accounts, credentials, and private course access are set out in the Terms of Service.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to:

- access your personal data.
- correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
- request deletion of your personal data.
- restrict certain processing.
- object to certain processing.
- request data portability.
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
- object to or opt out of marketing communications.
- lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

If you are in the European Union, you may contact your local supervisory authority. Where applicable, this may include the Hellenic Data Protection Authority in Greece.

To exercise privacy or GDPR rights, contact us at antonis@data-conscious.com.

The landing page may use essential cookies and similar technologies required for page and form functionality. We may also receive aggregate analytics from MailerLite, such as page views, unique visitors, form submissions, subscribers collected, and conversion rates.

We do not receive the email address or direct identifying details of every anonymous page visitor. We receive personal data when a user submits a form, subscribes, pays, contacts us, enrolls, requests support, or otherwise provides identifying information.

Where cookie settings or consent controls are available, you can manage them through the relevant website or service. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.
