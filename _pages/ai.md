<section class="brand-section" id="solutions" aria-labelledby="solutions-title">
<p class="eyebrow">What we can build together</p>
<h2 id="solutions-title">AI solutions connected to your work.</h2>
<div class="brand-grid two">
<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### Company knowledge & enterprise search

**Make company information easier to find and use.** An employee asks a question in everyday language. The assistant searches the connected company sources and returns an answer with references, so the employee can inspect the supporting information.

We build knowledge assistants and document Q&A systems over PDFs, knowledge repositories, and supported platforms such as SharePoint, Confluence, and Google Drive. The work includes document processing, indexing, hybrid keyword and semantic search, and relevance tuning.

Retrieval follows the access permissions agreed for the integration. We evaluate search relevance and answer quality against representative questions. Access rules and the handling of missing or conflicting information are defined during implementation.

*Illustrative use case: an operations employee asks which procedure applies to a particular request and receives the relevant guidance with a reference to the policy document.*


</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### AI agents & business process automation

**Move repetitive work forward with the right tools and oversight.** We combine conventional automation, APIs, and AI around defined business processes. Where a task needs to gather information or use several tools, a task-specific agent can prepare an output and pass it to the next step.

Use cases include email classification and routing, invoice and document processing, support ticket triage, data entry and validation, reporting workflows, and lead qualification.

Each workflow defines its inputs, validation rules, tool permissions, action limits, approval points, and responsible reviewers. We specify what happens when information is incomplete or a step fails. Monitoring and audit trails help the team understand what was processed and what still needs attention.

CRM and ERP integrations are scoped after reviewing the available APIs and access permissions. More complex agent workflows are considered when the use case justifies them.

*Illustrative use case: an incoming invoice is classified, the required fields are extracted, and agreed validation rules are applied. Complete records move to the next processing step; missing or inconsistent fields are sent to a reviewer with the reason they were flagged. Any connected-system update follows the agreed permissions and approval rules.*

</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### Conversational AI & assistants

**Help customers and employees get useful answers in context.** We design assistants around a defined set of user needs, such as finding product information, understanding a procedure, or preparing a support request.

Solutions include internal employee assistants, website and customer support assistants, product guidance, multilingual interfaces, and domain-specific copilots. Answers draw on the approved information connected to the assistant. We define its coverage, the information it needs to collect, and the handover process for questions that require a person. Responses are evaluated against the agreed scope.

*Illustrative use case: a website visitor asks about a product’s requirements. The assistant explains the documented requirements and, where further advice is needed, helps prepare a request for the support team.*


</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### AI for data & analytics

**Make business data easier to explore and explain.** We help teams ask questions of business data using agreed metric definitions and controlled access to the underlying datasets.

Solutions can include conversational analytics, natural-language-to-SQL with controlled database access, KPI exploration, business summaries, anomaly reporting, and explanations of data quality issues. The implementation establishes which questions the application can support, how generated queries are validated, and how results can be traced back to the figures used. Summaries distinguish observed changes from explanations that require further investigation.

*Illustrative use case: a manager asks how a KPI changed compared with the previous reporting period. The application returns the comparison using the agreed definition, shows the supporting breakdown, and identifies areas for further investigation.*


</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### Data engineering & business intelligence

**Build the data foundations that analytics and AI depend on.** We also offer data engineering and BI as standalone services, whether or not you are ready for an AI application.

We integrate databases, APIs, and business systems; build ingestion, cleaning, and transformation pipelines; define KPIs and data models; and prepare reliable data for dashboards and operational reporting. Depending on the scope, deliverables can include documented data pipelines, analytical models, agreed KPI definitions, data quality checks, and datasets prepared for dashboards or AI applications. We define how data is refreshed, how failures are surfaced, and who owns the resulting information, so the solution can be maintained after delivery.

*Illustrative use case: bring operational data into a consistent reporting model before building a dashboard or analytics assistant.*


</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### Evaluation, monitoring & ongoing improvement

**Keep AI applications useful after the prototype.** Before release, we evaluate the application against representative tasks and agreed acceptance criteria. For a knowledge assistant, these can include retrieval relevance, answer correctness, source support, and the handling of questions the system cannot answer.

Operational monitoring can cover failed requests, response times, usage, and model costs. Changes to documents, prompts, or models can then be assessed against the same evaluation cases. The work can also include logging, alerts, access controls, audit trails, and ongoing maintenance. Cloud, hybrid, or on-premises deployment options are assessed against your systems, privacy needs, and operational requirements.

*Illustrative use case: test a knowledge assistant against representative questions and track quality as documents and models change.*


</article>
