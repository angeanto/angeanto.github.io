<section class="brand-section" id="solutions" aria-labelledby="solutions-title">
<p class="eyebrow">What we can build together</p>
<h2 id="solutions-title">AI solutions connected to your work.</h2>
<div class="brand-grid two">

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### Company knowledge & enterprise search

**Make company information easier to find and use.**

Knowledge assistants and document Q&A over your company sources, with **source references**, **hybrid search**, and **permission-aware retrieval**. We handle document processing, indexing, and answer quality evaluation.

*Illustrative use case: Find the procedure for an operational request, with a reference to the relevant policy.*

</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### AI agents & business process automation

**Reduce repetitive work across your business tools.**

Automate **email routing**, **document processing**, **support triage**, and **reporting**. Use task-specific agents where needed, with human approvals and audit trails. CRM/ERP connections depend on available APIs and permissions.

*Illustrative use case: Extract invoice fields, validate them, and route exceptions to a reviewer before updating a connected system.*

</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### Conversational AI & assistants

**Help customers and employees get answers in context.**

Build **website assistants**, **internal copilots**, and **multilingual support** using approved business information. Define what the assistant can answer and when it should hand over to a person.

*Illustrative use case: Answer product questions from approved documentation and prepare a support request when further advice is needed.*

</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### AI for data & analytics

**Make business data easier to explore and explain.**

Enable **conversational analytics**, **natural-language-to-SQL**, **KPI exploration**, and **business summaries** over validated data. Use agreed metric definitions, controlled access, and query validation.

*Illustrative use case: Compare a KPI with the previous period and inspect the supporting figures.*

</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### Data engineering & business intelligence

**Build reliable foundations for analytics and AI.**

Connect **databases and APIs**, build **data pipelines**, define **KPIs and analytical models**, and prepare data for dashboards. Includes data quality checks and documentation; available as a standalone service.

*Illustrative use case: Bring operational data into a consistent reporting model before building a dashboard or analytics assistant.*

</article>

<article class="brand-card" markdown="1">

### Evaluation, monitoring & ongoing improvement

**Keep AI applications useful after the prototype.**

Test **retrieval relevance**, **answer correctness**, and **source support** against agreed criteria. Monitor errors, latency, and costs, and assess deployment options around your operational requirements.

*Illustrative use case: Test a knowledge assistant against representative questions and track quality as documents or models change.*

</article>
