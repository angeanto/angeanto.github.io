FAQ

¶

Is this course for beginners?

¶

Not really. You should have some SQL or analytics experience. You do not need to be senior already, but this is not the best first analytics course.

How technical is it?

¶

It is technical enough to be meaningful, but the goal is analyst growth, not platform administration. You will work with dbt, dlt, ClickHouse, PostgreSQL, and the surrounding ecosystem through guided labs.

Do I need to install the platform locally?

¶

No. The pilot uses a browser-based learner kit through GitHub Codespaces. You do not install Docker or run the full platform on your laptop.

Will I get access to the lab?

¶

Yes. Participants receive guided access to the learning environment, including personal ClickHouse databases and read-only access to the shared PostgreSQL Olist source through protected routes.

Will we use AI?

¶

Yes, responsibly. The course includes Wren AI exposure and AI-assisted analytics workflows, with emphasis on validation, privacy, assumptions, and human judgment.

Is this a dashboard course?

¶

No. BI is included, but the course is broader: architecture, pipelines, modeling, governance, metadata, AI, communication, and career positioning.

What happens if I miss a live session?

¶

The Moodle material and the recording remain available.

How much does it cost?

¶

The first cohort price is €400.

What does lifetime access include?

¶

Participants keep access to the fully updated Moodle LMS material for the course, including session resources, templates, exercises, supporting documents, and future LMS updates connected to the program.

Do I get any benefit for future DataConscious Labs?

¶

Yes. Participants in this cohort receive a 10% discount code for their next DataConscious Labs lab.

Will this help my CV?

¶

The final session is dedicated to CV and career positioning. You will learn how to describe your work more clearly and credibly. No course can guarantee interviews or offers.
